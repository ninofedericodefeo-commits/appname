# Subscription planning

Costs include real active subscriptions only, even in test mode. Next 7/30 days enumerate expected charges; annual run rate assumes 12 monthly charges or one annual charge at the saved price. Monthly equivalent divides that annual amount by 12, rounded to cents. A trial end is the expected first paid date. These views do not change Account or set aside money.

The calendar shows the saved planned schedule, including past dates in the selected month; it is not bank posting history. Monthly dates clamp to month-end without drifting the next month. Phone reminders exclude sample/canceled records and combine same-day events without merging two real subscriptions that happen to have the same name.

## Cancellation

Choose who bills you: Apple, Google Play, provider website, provider app, or Not sure. The supported installed-app picker remains available. Opening a destination records only the plan; the subscription stays active and remains included in costs. The plan has a persistent follow-up date, including after the reviewed renewal passes. Confirm `I canceled it` with a date to mark it canceled. Reactivation is explicit and retains cancellation history.

Phone reminders at 9 AM cover future cancellation due dates when the existing reminder preference and OS permission allow them. Due/overdue items remain visible in the app; a date whose 9 AM reminder time already passed does not promise a new phone alert. Opening a provider page does not confirm that cancellation happened.

Billing starting points were checked against these official instructions on October 14, 2026:

- [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428): iPhone account subscriptions; web/other platforms use the Apple Account site.
- [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481?hl=en): Google Play subscriptions with the paying account.
- [Spotify](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/cancel-premium/): account subscription management; partner-billed plans may require the partner.
- [Netflix](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/407): cancellation starting page; completion is still required.

Other associated apps retain their saved provider website fallback; a successful open is not evidence of the provider's current billing UI. Native link handoff and actual cancellation starting pages behind sign-in still need device acceptance.

## Bank review

Price changes and charges posted after a recorded cancellation remain review alerts. Possible refund/reversal credits are displayed separately and never silently clear the alert or reactivate a subscription. Explicit transfer/refund/reversal annotations exclude a debit from subscription evidence.

Billing-date/cadence review requires three newer stable posted charges. Delays of up to three calendar days are tolerated for the same cadence. A proposed schedule always goes through Edit and explicit Save; posting evidence never silently changes a saved renewal date. A saved schedule change establishes a new evidence anchor.
