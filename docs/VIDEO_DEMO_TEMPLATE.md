## Recording setup

- Use a standalone or preview build that opens reliably. Hide development menus and warnings. Turn on Do Not Disturb and remove personal notifications from the take.
- Pick one theme and a readable system text size. Keep the same device, orientation, and theme for the full video.
- Rehearse the actual installed build. Confirm the sample station details, Amazon handoff or paste fallback, sample statement **save**, and Bills screens all work before recording.
- Preload the search results and prepare the sample PDF in Files. Screen recordings that cross into Files or Amazon must contain only test/public material.
- Record each scene as a separate clip named `01-opening` through `10-closing`. Leave two seconds before and after each action for editing. Export the final timeline at **00:03:00**.

## Editing pattern

Use simple cuts and occasional short dissolves. Let one action finish before the next starts. Hold important results for two to three seconds; avoid rapid scrolling. Add a subtle tap indicator where it helps viewers follow a control. Keep music quiet under the narration and add synchronized captions.

Keep sample disclosures visible whenever fictional figures are on screen. A sample map must never be presented as evidence of live fuel prices. Do not composite a successful import or price lookup over a failed recording.

## Swaps that preserve the runtime

Replace a scene with one of **the same duration** rather than extending the video:

- **Amazon share extension unavailable:** use the 20-second paste-link fallback already described in scene 5. Do not imply that native sharing was demonstrated.
- **Live station results unavailable:** show the labeled Haddonfield samples and filter controls in scene 2. Do not describe the sample map as live search results.
- **Wallet emphasis:** replace the 22-second Bills scene with Wallet setup and inbox review **only after verifying the installed native action on an iPhone**. Suggested narration: “A configured Wallet automation can queue supported taps for review without opening GasFinder at checkout. Open the app later, check the merchant and amount, and save the purchase. Queuing alone doesn’t update your account.” Do not claim every purchase is captured or imply an untested real-payment recording.
- **Different audience:** retain the scene lengths and replace the goal, bill example, and closing action. Refresh any visible dates or prices and record a new matching voiceover.
