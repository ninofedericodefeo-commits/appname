# Haddonfield station examples

The sample map uses two real public station locations:

| Station | Address | Coordinates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunoco, Haddonfield | 515 Haddon Ave, Haddonfield, NJ 08033 | 39.90359, -75.04012 |
| Sunoco, Kings Highway | 1605 N Kings Hwy, Cherry Hill, NJ 08034 | 39.91604, -75.00771 |

The addresses and exact latitude/longitude pairs were checked against Sunoco's public location pages and their embedded station coordinates on October 18, 2026: [Haddonfield](https://www.sunoco.com/locations/store/515-haddon-ave-haddonfield-nj-0004657308) and [Cherry Hill](https://www.sunoco.com/locations/store/1605-n-kings-hwy-cherry-hill-nj-0697019801). Cherry Hill is explicitly labeled as near Haddonfield.

Fuel prices, price histories, fill-ups and comparison assumptions remain synthetic. They are labeled Sample in cards, map markers and details. These fixtures never enter live station searches, price reporting, favorites, account records or caches. The map does not request device location. OpenStreetMap attribution is retained; map tiles require internet, while the fixture cards remain available offline.

Selecting a sample marker opens its matching sample detail page. The shared sample visibility toggle removes both maps and details. Internal sample IDs are retained so existing preview links continue to work.
