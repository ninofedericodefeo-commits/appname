# Physical phone smoke test

[Expo SDK 57](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/) supports Android 7+ and iOS 16.4+. Record the exact device model, OS version, build identifier, tester, and date for each run. The first supported test devices and launch region still need a product decision.

For an iPhone signed by a free Apple Personal Team, regenerate the iOS project after changing native configuration, then install it while the phone is unlocked:

```bash
npm run ios -- --device
```

This command regenerates native configuration before building. With Metro already running on 8081, add `--no-bundler`; do not combine it with `--port`. Keep Metro running with `npx expo start --host lan` while using a development build. This Personal Team configuration keeps local subscription reminders but omits Push Notifications and the iPhone goal widget because its App Group cannot be provisioned. Builds signed by an enrolled Apple Developer Program team can opt back into both capabilities with `EXPO_ENABLE_IOS_CAPABILITIES=1` during prebuild and build.

1. Install a development build on each agreed Android and iOS device using the project’s EAS setup (`npx eas-cli@latest build --profile development --platform android|ios` after EAS configuration and sign-in).
2. Open, reload, close, and reopen the app. Confirm the Gas screen immediately requests foreground location access and starts loading real nearby stations. On later opens with permission already granted, it should begin searching without another tap.
3. Grant location, deny it, disable device location services, and change permission later in Settings. Confirm each state is explained and sample data is never shown as an online result.
4. Test fuel, radius, and sort selections; a station with no chosen-fuel price; a stale price; an empty response; and a backend timeout. Confirm displayed distance and lowest-price card badge change correctly.
5. Confirm the bottom bar has four tabs: Gas, Goals, Account, and Subs. Goals has its goal and past goals; Account has the unified transaction history; Subs has subscriptions and Spending pause. Activity is removed. Open and close inline create/edit forms; hide and reopen a draft, change bottom tabs, and confirm drafts remain. Tab changes should have no push animation or growing back stack. `/spending` opens Subs and `/activity` opens Transactions. Logging a bought pause item opens Transactions with its purchase. Detail screens slide in and return to their opener. Test small screens, large text, keyboards and touch targets.
6. Confirm the previous investing demo appears only in the read-only archive under Goals → Past goals and never counts toward a new goal.
7. Add, skip, and remove a spending-pause entry. Confirm the reported avoided-spending estimate updates and no notification or payment-blocking claim appears.
8. Test with slow and no connectivity. Confirm map/search failures have Retry and List fallback, and no sample prices are substituted.
9. Open receipts from Gas and from a real station card. Take a receipt photo and choose one from the photo library. Confirm price, station name, fuel, purchase date, and mapped location or address. Attach GPS only while at the station; test location denial and manual address entry. Save, restart, check the report, photo, and linked station price, then delete the report and confirm the linked price disappears.
9a. From Gas, report a price without a receipt. Confirm price is first, then fuel type, then the GPS yes/no choice. With GPS, check that coordinates and an approximate place name/address appear in the faded panel. Test denial, manual details, edit, and delete. From a mapped station, report a price and confirm it appears on that station and in its local history graph. Confirm changing coordinates removes the mapped link. An unnamed map station should say **Name not listed**. A station without a real price should show **No price yet** and must not become the lowest price. On a slow connection, check that stations within 2 miles appear while the wider search continues.
9b. Confirm Gas defaults to **Map**, the green sample-data banner is absent, and Report a price / Gas on your drive sit below it. Check the modern vector streets, rounded price markers, location dot, recenter and zoom controls, and required attribution. Unknown prices show Gas. Switch to List and back, tap markers, and check directions/report/history. Pan and select another marker: selection must preserve the camera. On slow connectivity, markers arrive with the first results. Turn off connectivity: a map warning with Retry and usable List must remain. Verify iPhone/Android WebGL/module-worker loading, pinch zoom, page scrolling and screen-reader access. On a planned drive, check numbered stops/blue route, changed routes, and no leftover line when a route is cleared.
9c. Open **Gas on your drive → Manage cars**. Add Subaru Crosstrek and a second model, rename each, edit MPG and tank capacity, select either, restart, and verify persistence. Test an unmatched/older custom car and invalid MPG/capacity. Confirm tank size is explicitly a guess. Test switching cars and editing a profile during/after planning; the old plan must clear and a new car asks for its fuel level.
9d. First enter a destination address/city in the app and plan from it. Then optionally share/paste a destination from Apple/Google Maps, including a short link, a full destination URL and coordinates. On free-team iPhone test the documented share Shortcut; on Android test direct text sharing; on an enrolled-team iPhone build test the Expo Sharing extension. Confirm a second shared destination replaces the first, and a cold-launch/hot-launch works for the installed build. Verify a saved destination is remembered but never claimed to be read automatically from Maps. Test invalid links and refused clipboard access.
9e. In Settings choose 10% refill level, then 25%, and confirm both persist after restart. Verify the refill target uses full tank capacity, not a fraction of remaining fuel, and changing it invalidates an existing plan. Start below the refill level and verify a nearby reachable stop is preferred. Select a car and enter **only** percent or gallons remaining. Test percent presets, manual percentage, zero/above-100 values, and gallons exceeding tank capacity. Plan trips with enough fuel, low initial fuel and a drive long enough for at least three refills. Confirm every stop is within estimated range, each next search starts from the selected stop, and every stop says refill to full. Check stops appear progressively. Simulate a later missing stop and service timeout: earlier stops remain but the remaining trip is explicitly uncovered and whole-trip export is unavailable. Test Cancel and changing inputs during a request.
9f. In Settings select Apple or Google Maps and verify station cards and trip handoff honor it; Android uses Google. Open the full trip in Apple Maps on iOS 18.4+ and confirm the destination plus all waypoints. On older iOS use per-stop directions. Test Google Maps with more than three waypoints: numbered parts must preserve every stop and end at the destination. Check those routes before driving. Check an optional stop on a trip with enough fuel using **Find gas on this trip anyway**.
9g. Open the keyboard in destination, car nickname, MPG, refill percentage, receipt fields, goals and modal purchase entry. Move between fields with the keyboard open, check the focused input stays above it, drag to dismiss and verify the tab bar returns. Test both iPhone and Android.
10. In Account, confirm the title stays below the phone time/status bar, editors start closed, and the actual balance starts masked. There must be one Import statement button. Save a balance, use Manage savings to set aside/lower money, and confirm Available to spend equals the dated balance estimate minus money set aside. Newer posted/logged activity should update the estimate once; the confirmed Last bank balance stays unchanged until a balance choice/manual update. Reveal/hide the smaller bank balance and restart: it is masked again, values persist. Test large amounts/text and negative available balance. Reserve/release adjustments appear in Transactions with a Savings label and Local only details, without suggesting money moved at the bank.
11. Add monthly and yearly subscriptions with prices. Test a renewal on the first of a month, a date on the 31st, edit, mark canceled, reactivate, and delete. On the day before renewal, confirm the review asks about each charge and the total. Choose Keep and Plan to cancel; confirm that the latter clearly says to cancel with the provider. Open universal Settings → Phone reminders, enable subscription renewals, grant permission, and confirm a 9 AM local notification is scheduled; disable reminders and confirm scheduled entries are cleared.
11a. From Account, tap **Import statement**, or use **Import bank PDF or CSV** in Subs. Choose a downloaded Citizens checking/savings statement with its full statement period (not a scan); compare every extracted amount/description/date and page/line with the PDF. Verify that deposits, transfers, card payments, fees, checks and summary balances are not counted as purchases. Import history-only first, then test the optional capped goal rule. Rename the linked account and reimport overlapping statements and confirm known rows cannot be selected. Cancel reading or leave the screen during import and check no late result is saved. Try offline extraction, an unsupported bank/card PDF, password-protected/scanned PDF, unknown layout, unreadable row and oversized file. Recheck CSV file selection and the old-build fallback. Never commit a real statement or its contents.
11b. Leave **Save bank history to Account** enabled and review signed deposits, transfers, fees and supplied pending/posted status. Reimport an older statement with existing purchases: bank history may be added, purchases must not be added again. Account should show each linked charge once, newest first, with source/account details on tap. See full history: test search, Money in, Money out, Pending and Savings, including an empty filter. Reimport again and confirm no new/updated rows; saving the original file remains available. CSV imports leave the saved balance unchanged; a detected PDF closing balance requires an explicit Use PDF balance or Keep current balance answer; only Use PDF balance updates the dated snapshot. Turn off bank history once and confirm only selected purchases are imported. CSV without status must not invent posted/pending state; savings review decisions must not label bank charges pending. Verify scrolling, keyboard and screen reader behavior on both platforms. Expand a linked purchase for Edit/delete and savings review; editing/deleting its savings record must preserve the original statement movement. Standalone purchases show the same controls. Log a purchase from Transactions without an active goal, then test suggestions, custom amounts and skipping with an active goal. Purchase contributions appear inside their transaction; standalone adjustments appear separately.
11c. Import several months from the same account. Open **Subs → From bank history**; verify recurring monthly/yearly charges and their supporting dates/prices. A single known or explicitly recurring charge must be marked possible. Tap **Review & add**, edit the price and confirm the renewal day with the provider, then add. It must persist on restart without a second suggestion. Dismiss another suggestion, restart, restore it, and confirm existing/renamed/canceled subscriptions stay suppressed. No import may enable notifications or change savings unless the goal rule was selected. Check screen readers, large text and keyboard handling on both platforms.
12. Create an untimed goal, then a timed goal with a future date selected from the calendar. At setup, choose fixed dollars, percent, and round-up rules in turn. Test title, target, date, and rule edits using Edit goal; Goal type should not appear. Start with money already earmarked in the set-aside balance and test Assign none and Assign all. Confirm no money is added to the set-aside balance by assignment. Lower goal money inside Edit goal, delete an active goal with confirmation, and verify its money remains unassigned in Account.
13. Log three actual purchases and confirm the chosen rule automatically updates goal progress and the set-aside balance estimate, capped by the goal remainder and entered balance. Test a rule that yields zero so the pending suggestion flow appears; suggestions should adapt, respect the $5 and 10% defaults, and respond to limit changes. Save one suggestion, skip another, and use a custom amount. Edit and delete a purchase; confirmed goal money must remain until explicitly lowered in Edit goal.
13a. On iPhone, configure a Shortcuts Transaction automation for a selected Apple Pay card, passing Amount and URL-encoded Merchant to `gasfinder://import-purchase`. Make an eligible card tap and verify it logs once and applies the rule. Repeat the same link to check duplicate prevention. Test a zero/invalid amount. Confirm other purchases are not presented as automatically captured.
14. Mark a Spending Pause item as bought. Log the actual amount and confirm the purchase appears only once in Transactions. Also test Bought without logging. Confirm gas receipts do not create goal purchases.
15. Add and lower goal money through Edit goal, restart, and confirm goal and set-aside totals agree. Finish a goal, inspect Past goals, then create another with Assign all or Assign none. Lowering unassigned savings must never use money assigned to the active goal.
15a. In Goals open **Goal from Amazon link**. Type/paste a direct product link and an a.co short link; check clipboard permission denial and manual paste recovery. Reject a misleading hostname, HTTP link and unsupported marketplace. Verify that a readable product fills in its name, selected variant and full USD price. Confirm the sale price wins over a crossed-out list price, and never use coupons, monthly payments, ranges or other currencies. Test blocked/offline/unavailable and name-only results with retry/manual fallback. Cancel a pending lookup, change its URL, or leave the screen and verify no late response changes the draft. Review/edit the details, choose a deadline/rule, and cancel without changing savings. Create with Assign none, then test Assign all after confirmed archive. Restart, check the source link and variant, edit the target, and confirm saved money stays unchanged. Open the source in the Amazon app or browser and return. Check small screens, large text, VoiceOver/TalkBack and keyboard handling on both platforms. Imported names/prices must match the selected offer and show Amazon price provenance/read date; editing the price must mark it as manual. Images are not imported. Web production requires a deployed API server; native lookup does not.
16. On an iPhone build signed by an enrolled Apple Developer Program team with `EXPO_ENABLE_IOS_CAPABILITIES=1`, enable the Savings goal widget in settings, add it to the Home and Lock Screens, and confirm progress changes after setting money aside or reopening the app. The default widget should show only a generic title and percent; enable Show amounts and title to verify the other display. Disable the widget and confirm it hides goal details. On a free Personal Team build, confirm the widget is unavailable and goal progress remains visible inside the app. Check the in-app progress on Android.

For each failure, record steps, expected and actual result, severity, and a screenshot or recording if useful. Do not enter payment credentials or use customer funds.
