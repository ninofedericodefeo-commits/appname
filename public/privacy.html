Centsward privacy policy

Draft — not published

Updated October 22, 2026

This draft describes the current private version of Centsward, a personal money-management app with optional gas search and shopping tools. Publisher identity and a privacy contact have not been supplied. Review and complete this policy before distributing the app.

Your financial records stay on your device

The app saves your account name, balances, transactions, goals, bills, budgets, cars, gas receipts, statement imports and preferences in local app storage. It does not require a Centsward account or your bank login. Statements and receipt images are parsed on your device; the current app does not upload them to a financial-data service.

Optional device authentication uses the operating system. The app receives an authentication result, not your fingerprint or Face ID template. App lock and hidden amounts protect access and display; they do not add encryption to the local ledger. Password-encrypted exported backups have separate protection.

Home Screen widgets can display selected balances or goal information outside the app. Use widget privacy settings to control this. Local reminders may display bill or statement information in notifications, depending on your device notification settings.

Optional online features

Gas search uses your location when you grant permission, or the area you enter. Station services receive the search area; address search services receive the place or address you type; route services receive the starting point, destination and any waypoints. Map tile services receive requests for the map areas you view. These requests also expose ordinary network information such as your IP address.

Default services include OpenStreetMap tiles, Overpass station search, Photon address search and OSRM routing. A developer-configured station service may instead process the station request. Opening directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps shares the destination or route with that service.

On iPhone and Android, Amazon product lookup opens the listing directly in a temporary in-app browser that does not share cookies with your normal browser, so Amazon receives the listing request and network information. On the web, the product link is sent to the app’s lookup server, which requests the listing from Amazon. It does not send your statements, account balance or entire purchase history. Opening a shop or bill-provider link hands you to that website or app, which applies its own privacy practices.

These services may keep request or server logs under their own policies. Their retention and the final hosting configuration have not been verified for public distribution. This draft does not promise that online requests leave no records. Financial records and gas-price reports saved through the current app interface are not automatically synchronized between phones.

Purchase capture and browser companion

If you configure the optional iPhone Wallet automation, Shortcuts can queue a merchant name, amount, currency and time for review. A supplied transaction identifier is converted into a local deduplication identifier. The app does not receive your card number or bank credentials through this feature. Pending drafts expire after 30 days and the queue holds at most 200 drafts. The native queue is excluded from device backups; saved transactions follow normal local-record retention.

The optional spending-pause browser extension stores your selected shops, pause times and overrides in browser-local storage. It applies checkout-blocking rules for selected sites. Its code does not upload your browsing history or checkout activity to the app developer. Disabling or removing the extension stops its blocking; its settings are separate from the app’s stored financial records.

The iPhone app can check whether supported bill-provider apps can open; it cannot list every installed app. The Android version can inspect launchable apps to help identify supported providers. This matching stays on the device.

Files, sharing and device backups

You choose which statements, receipt photos or files to import. Exporting a statement report, transaction CSV or encrypted backup gives the file to the destination you select. Files can contain sensitive financial information. Copies you save to Files, cloud storage, email or another app are handled by that destination and are not removed when you erase Centsward.

Local app data may be included in operating-system or device backups, subject to your device settings. Browser users share the privacy boundary of their browser profile. Do not rely on hidden amounts or app lock to protect an exported file or a shared browser profile.

Retention and deletion

Saved financial records and preferences remain until you change or remove them, erase the app’s data, or remove the app, subject to device backups. Archived statement imports can remain in Trash until permanently deleted. Network-service logs and exported copies have separate retention controlled by their operators or by you.

Use Settings → Data & backups → Erase all app data to remove managed records, imported files, receipt copies, drafts and the local purchase inbox. Some device-protection preferences remain configured. Remove exported copies and device backups separately. Manage browser-extension storage and widget visibility separately as needed.

Permissions and your choices

Location is optional for nearby gas search; enter an area instead. Camera and photo access support receipts you choose. File access supports imports and exports you request. Notifications support local reminders. Device authentication supports the optional app lock. Revoke permissions in your device settings at any time; the related feature may then be unavailable.

Online maps, routing, product lookup and external links need network services. You can continue using saved financial records without invoking those services. The current app code includes no advertising SDK, cross-app advertising tracking or analytics upload of your financial records.

Privacy questions and future changes

Publisher: to be supplied before publication. Privacy contact: to be supplied before publication. This private draft has no public privacy-request channel yet. Do not send statements or card details to an unverified contact.

When the app’s data practices change, update this policy and its date, review the store disclosures and make the revised policy available in the app and at the public policy address. This draft has not been submitted to App Store Connect.
